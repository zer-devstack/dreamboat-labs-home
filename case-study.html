What we built

One platform, many products

What started as a single ordering tool is now a set of connected apps for everyone in the supply chain.

Marketplace for participants and families

Browse and order consumables, scheduled repeat orders, NDIS budget tracking with low-funding alerts, and live delivery tracking.

Stripe online payments

Pay Now links sent by SMS or email, pharmacy subscription plans and paid add-ons, all handled through Stripe.

SMS notifications and automation

Two-way SMS with customers, ready-to-collect and driver-on-the-way texts, plus scheduled orders, reminders and remittances that run on their own.

Medication management

Patient profiles, medication packing workflows, printable packing lists and ready-to-collect texts for patients.

Deliveries and driver app

Route planning, a driver app that works with patchy signal, proof of delivery with photos and signatures, and customer SMS updates.

POS and accounting integrations

A live link to the pharmacy's point-of-sale system for patients, dispensing history and balances, plus invoices, bills and remittances synced to Xero or MYOB.

AI built in
