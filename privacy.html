Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Dreamboat Labs ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it and how we look after it. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles and the New Zealand Privacy Act.

What we collect

  • Your first name, work email and product or company name, when you complete the AI Scorecard.
  • Your scorecard answers and result.
  • Anything you choose to send us by email, WhatsApp or LinkedIn.
  • Basic, anonymous website usage data (such as pages visited), if we use analytics.

Why we collect it

  • To send you your scorecard results and a few short follow-up emails.
  • To reply to your messages and talk with you about working together.
  • To improve our website and the scorecard.

How we store and share it

We store your details in secure, access-controlled tools such as Google Workspace and Gmail. These providers may store data outside Australia and New Zealand. We never sell your personal information, and we only share it with service providers who help us run this website and send emails.

Emails and unsubscribing

We only email you after you've taken the scorecard or contacted us. Every email includes a way to opt out: just reply "stop" and we'll remove you.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Email us and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Contact us

Questions or concerns about your privacy? Email zer@dreamboat-labs.com. Dreamboat Labs is based in the Philippines.
