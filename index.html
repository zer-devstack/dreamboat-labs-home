FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Can't see your question? Send Zer a message and we'll answer it.

Do I own the code?+

Yes. Everything we build for you is yours: the code, the designs and the documentation. It lives in your accounts, not ours.

Can you work with my current developer or agency?+

Yes. We can lead and review their work, work alongside them, or take over step by step. Whatever keeps your product moving with the least disruption.

How do you keep our data safe?+

We only use the access we need, with secure logins, and your data stays in your own hosting. We already run a platform that handles sensitive health data in Australia, so we take this seriously.

Is it safe to use AI on our product?+

We use AI to work faster, and a senior lead checks every change before it goes live. We never put your customers' personal information into AI tools without your approval.

What does it cost to get started?+

Every engagement starts with a short paid review, credited toward your project and backed by a money-back guarantee. Message Zer and we'll share the details.

What if it doesn't work out?+

There are no long contracts. Ongoing work runs month to month, and you keep everything we've built.

Will the time difference be a problem?+

No. We're in the Philippines, only 2–5 hours behind Australia and New Zealand, so we work your business day and reply the same day.
